# Church-affiliated learning centers we studied

Patterns only. Do not copy their copy, staff, or invented programs onto SonShine.

| Site | Why it works | What we took |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Christ Lutheran School](https://school.christlutheran.us/) (North St. Paul, MN) | One persistent CTA (“Book a tour”) on every page. Real classroom photos. Parent-first nav. NAP in the footer. | Header **Enroll** button. Real SonShine photos, not stock kids. |
| [PCCH Preschool](https://pcchpreschool.org/) (Philadelphia) | Warm and professional. Classrooms named clearly. Parents can find forms without hunting. | Short parent nav: Enroll, Schedule, Teachers. Forms as first-class files. |
| [Calvary Chapel Schools preschool](https://www.calvaryschools.org/academic/preschool/) (CA) | Ages and days named in the first screen. Tour as the action. Distinct from the church homepage. | Lead with ages 3–4, M/W/F, 9–12. Keep this domain separate from calvaryfrederick.com. |
| [The Way PDO](https://blaksheepcreative.com/portfolio/the-way-pdo-preschool-web-refresh/) | StoryBrand: who it’s for, how a day works, next step. | Purpose / typical morning / email Mrs. Mhairi. |
| [Good Shepherd Lutheran Preschool](https://www.evokexperts.com/blog/gslc-preschool) (Maryland) | Sibling brand to the church: distinct, still connected. | Teal family with gold preschool accent. Footer “ministry of CCF.” |

Anti-patterns we skipped: mega PK–8 sitemaps, fake testimonials, lorem camp dates, leftover `/cart`, empty meta descriptions.
